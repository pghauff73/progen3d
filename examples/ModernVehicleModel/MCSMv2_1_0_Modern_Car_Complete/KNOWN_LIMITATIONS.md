# MCSMv2.1 Known Limitations

MCSMv2.1 is a V2 concept-geometry release.

- Registration aligns an explicit semantic mesh to a separate implicit scaffold, but it is not production Class-A NURBS surfacing.
- Panel domains are exclusive surface partitions. They do not yet contain physical gaps, inner panels, hems, flanges, seals, hinges or latches.
- Glass aperture supports are static surface patches. Side windows do not yet follow barrel surfaces or helical drop paths.
- Wheelhouse geometry still uses conservative swept-envelope approximations rather than exact suspension-hardpoint kinematics.
- BIW validation remains a concept graph and does not prove geometric joints, stiffness, crash performance or manufacturability.
- The inverse fit remains a synthetic semantic-target demonstration and does not establish parameter identifiability from calibrated images.
- No CFD, wind-tunnel, crash, thermal, ergonomic, stamping, homologation or physical test claim is made.
