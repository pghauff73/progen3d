# Quality and evidence

Visual match is the minimum historical score of recorded front, right and top view comparisons where a matching repository record exists. It is not an engineering certification and has not been rerun. Missing measurements are unmeasured.

Quality evidence rating (0–100) measures evidence completeness, not visual or mechanical fidelity: source available 35; description and usage documented 20; published source-related image or labelled source-derived preview 25; recorded per-object numeric match 20. Source-derived previews are diagnostic projections, not native renderer results. Engineering, material, dimensional, motion, parser and manufacturing tests require independent acceptance gates.
