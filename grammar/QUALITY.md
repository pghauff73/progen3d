# Quality and evidence

Two separate measurements are reported. **Visual match** is the minimum historical score of recorded front, right and top view comparisons where a matching repository evidence record exists. It is not an engineering certification and has not been rerun. Missing measurements are reported as unmeasured.

**Quality evidence rating** (0–100) is an evidence-completeness indicator, not a visual or mechanical fidelity estimate: exact source available 35; description and usage documented 20; source-related published render available 25; recorded per-object numeric match 20. Published images are explicitly qualified when exact correspondence is not verified. No image or score is fabricated. Engineering, material, dimensional, motion, parser, and manufacturing tests need independent acceptance gates.

Future evaluation: independently score parser validity, geometry accuracy, visual fidelity, parameterisation, physical validation and reproducibility. Use N/A for missing evidence, not a fabricated pass. Maintain source hashes and renderer revision.
