Source notesDucted fan with a steerable internal passage · Native ProGen3D grammar. One model unit represents one millimetre. · Edit Start: bore radius, nominal tip gap, blade count, outlet angle (degrees), · outside radius, minimum radial wall, show casing (1 or 0). · Try a straight duct with angle 0, reverse the bend with -18, or widen the bore to 55. · Admitted envelope: bore 40..60 mm, gap 0.25..3 mm, 3..10 whole blades, · bend -30..30 degrees, positive minimum wall. The outer radius must clear the passage. · Rotor animation: t in seconds, one illustrative revolution every 6 seconds. · The casing's inscribed radius bounds the wall even between polygon vertices. · The largest bore extent occurs at the inlet lip or at the far side of the exit.
